> **Резиме (за сопственикот).** ДДВ веќе не е 18 % за сè. Секој производ го носи ДДВ што го наплаќаат
> нашите книги (Сигма): **додатоци во исхраната и храна 5 %**, **козметика, гелови, креми, масла, уреди и
> чиа-пијалаци 18 %**. Секоја продажба се оданочува по ставка, по стапката на својот производ. Што нема
> стапка (MEX пратки без нарачка, ставки без производ, нов производ) се пресметува со 5 % и се прикажува

## 1. The decision

- **01.10.2026, Mile (owner side):** the CRM's flat 18 % VAT is wrong. Every product carries the rate
  Natura's own books (the Sigma ERP) charge for it. This **replaces** the ruling of 28.09.2026 ("VAT: 18 % —
  CONFIRMED by the owner"), `VAT_RATE = 0.18` in `supabase/functions/api/index.ts` and `VAT_CONFIRMED` in
  `insightsProfit.ts` — all three are gone.
- **Our systems hold the CORRECT rate, even where Sigma itself is wrong** (owner, 01.10): the table in
  `docs/vat/crm_products_vat.json` is that corrected rate. Where it overrules Sigma, `vat_source` and
  `vat_evidence` say so, so every row stays traceable; Sigma's own errors are reported separately.

## 2. The evidence

- **Sigma item master** (`Item.csv`, read-only export 29–30.09.2026): every item carries `VatId` —
  `0` = 0 %, `1` = **18 %**, `2` = **5 %**, `3` = 10 %. Every food supplement is `VatId 2`; cosmetics, gels,
  creams, oils, devices and the chia drinks are `VatId 1`.
- **Sigma sales invoices** (`WorkDocInLine.csv`, document type ПН1, every line with the VAT actually

- **The MEX COD invoice example:** MAGNESIUM BISGLYCINATE on the July 2026 МЕКС ПОШТА invoice —
  **476,19 net + 23,81 VAT (5 %) = 500,00**.
- The per-product table with its evidence: [`docs/vat/crm_products_vat.json`](vat/crm_products_vat.json)
  (and `.csv`), the Sigma items ([`docs/vat/sigma_items_vat.csv`](vat/sigma_items_vat.csv)), the rows a human

## 3. The method — `products.vat_source`

| `vat_source` | How the rate was decided | 01.10.2026 (706 products) |
|---|---|---|
| `sigma:crosswalk-VERIFIED` / `-HIGH` / `-MEDIUM` | the S2 product crosswalk links the product to a Sigma item; rate = that item's `VatId` | 395 at 5 % · 138 at 18 % |
| `sigma:manual` | the crosswalk link was wrong and was corrected by hand (e.g. ELIXY Vitamin C serum was linked to vitamin C tablets) | — (storefront rows) |
| `sigma:by-name` | no crosswalk link; the product's name names a Sigma item (the earliest named wins: a bundle takes its main product's rate) | 17 at 5 % · 9 at 18 % |
| `sigma:by-name+mixed` | as above, and the name also names items of the other rate (a supplement bundle with a cosmetic gift) — the main product's rate | 3 at 5 % · 24 at 18 % |
| `rule:supplement-5` / `rule:cosmetic-18` (`+mixed`) / `rule:device-18` | no Sigma item at all — by product type | 16 at 5 % · 84 + 20 at 18 % |
| `owner` | set in the CRM by an owner (Производи → the ДДВ chip), audited | — |

Totals: **431 products at 5 %, 275 at 18 %**; of the products sold since 2025, **190 at 5 % and 47 at 18 %**.
`vat_sigma_code` / `vat_sigma_name` = the Sigma item; `vat_evidence` = its 2025–2026 sales-invoice lines by
year and rate (`2026@5.00: 77; mex@5.00: 10` — `mex@` = the МЕКС ПОШТА invoices), or an owner's note.

## 4. How the CRM uses it

- **The data** (migration `20260944000900_product_vat_rate.sql`): `products.vat_rate numeric(4,3)` CHECK
  (0, 0.05, 0.10, 0.18), `vat_source`, `vat_sigma_code`, `vat_sigma_name`, `vat_evidence`, `vat_set_by`,
  `vat_set_at`. The 706 products of 01.10 are backfilled from the table (generated by
  `scripts/vat/gen-vat-backfill.mjs`; `vatRates.test.ts` checks the block against the JSON row by row).
- **One audited writer** (the `brand_line` / `kind` pattern): `products_set_vat_rate(ids, rate, actor, note)`
  behind the guard trigger `trg_products_vat_guard` — any other write of the seven columns is refused (42501);
  one `audit_log` row `products.set_vat_rate` per call with every change (from / to / previous source). API:
  `POST /api/products/vat-rate {ids, rate, note?}` — **owners only**. `PATCH /products/:id` drops the columns.
- **Who sees it:** owners (`is_business_owner()`) — `GET /products` and `/products/catalogue` strip the VAT
  columns for everyone else (the rate is part of the margins view, like the cost price).
- **Pure Profit / Margins** (`insights_profit()`, `insightsProfit.ts`): VAT **per line** — each sale's value is
  already split over its lines by price weight (`rv`); VAT = Σ rv × r/(1+r), r = the line's product rate. New
  measures per aggregate row: `vt` (VAT), `vc` (VAT of the costed packages — the waterfall's "costed" basis),
  `vu` (value with no rate), `v00`/`v05`/`v10`/`v18` (value by rate); per product `vt`, `vr` (rate), `vd`
  (defaulted); the payload says `vat_mode: 'per_line'`. Every other number is unchanged (proved: §6).
  The api: `meta.vat = {mode: 'per_product_sigma', default_rate: 0.05, by_rate, effective_rate, unclassified}`;
  each P&L row carries `vat_split` (5 % / 18 %), `vat_unclassified`, `vat_costed_mkd`; each product
  `vat_rate` and `vat_classified`. The waterfall's VAT line reads **"ДДВ по производ (Сигма)"** with its
  5 % / 18 % parts; the statement by department shows the VAT line by rate and its unclassified part; the
  product table has a ДДВ column (rate under the amount); the quality rail lists **"Продажби без стапка на
  ДДВ од Сигма"** with the biggest ones; the export has the VAT by rate and per product.
- **The floor price and the simulator** (`profitModel.ts`): `P = (1 + r)(target + cost + courier +
  commission)` with **the product's** r; the simulator starts at the product's rate (an owner may try another).
- **The legacy `/management-insights` blocks** (no UI reads them; the parity harness does): VAT per product
  **name** (`vatRateByName`, `vatOfNamedRevenue` in `vatRates.ts`) on the paid cash; the channel rows get the
  window's per-product VAT share of their cash (an allocation — those rows carry no product split).
- **The monthly profit cache**: `insights_profit_cache_version()` 4 → **5** (nothing cached with the flat 18 %
  is ever merged) and `insights_profit_cache_sig()` includes every product's `vat_rate` (an owner's change
  invalidates the cached months; the nightly refresh rebuilds them).

### Unclassified and new products

- A product created after 01.10.2026 has `vat_rate = NULL` = **Некласифицирано**. Nothing guesses a rate
  into it. Every report taxes it at **5 %** (the core range) and shows it apart: the P&L's
  `vat_unclassified`, the quality rail, the product table ("нема стапка од Сигма — по 5%"). The owner sets it
  on /products (the ДДВ chip, or select products → "Постави ДДВ"; the ДДВ filter row finds the
  unclassified ones).
- Lines with no product at all are unclassified too: **MEX parcels without an order** (contents unknown —
  454.771 ден in September), sales without lines, names the catalogue does not know (web lines no alias maps),
  the **ДОСТАВА** delivery-charge lines (13.777 ден in September).

## 5. Open questions for the accountant (not data questions)

1. **Is 5 % legally right for food supplements?** The books apply it throughout 2025–2026; the CRM follows
   the books.
2. **The monthly МЕКС ПОШТА COD invoices value the boxes at a few FIXED price tiers incl. VAT** (mostly
   495 / 295 / 195 / 100 / 60 ден; АД Астра's 004 series 500 / 600 / 900 / 1.000 / 1.200 ден), **not at what
   each customer paid**, and gift boxes are invoiced too; АД Астра's 004 invoices carried **0 %** before its VAT
   registration (mid-2026). The CRM taxes what the customer paid, at the product's rate.
3. **Mixed bundles** (a supplement with a cosmetic gift, e.g. "2 COLLAGEN … + ELIXY Matcha Face Cream + …
   MASK") carry their main product's rate; strictly, each component has its own rate.
4. **The delivery charge** ("ДОСТАВА" lines) is taxed at the default 5 %; a delivery service may be 18 %.

**Sigma's own VAT problems** (the CRM holds the correct rate per product regardless) are listed in full in
`D:\naturatherapy\vat-sigma\Сигма_ДДВ_неправилности_2026-10-01.xlsx` (built by
`D:\naturatherapy\vat-sigma\sigma_vat_anomalies.py`; counts in
[`docs/vat/sigma_vat_anomalies_summary.json`](vat/sigma_vat_anomalies_summary.json)): **9 items with a rate
wrong for their kind** (6 mixed supplement + cosmetic sets at 5 %, the SKIN GLOW serum and АЛОЕ ВЕРА ГЕЛ 250 at
5 %, 2 loyalty tablets at 5 %, PROSTA COMPLEKS at 0 %), **72 invoice lines of 2025–26 charged at a different
rate than the item** (50 of them on MEX invoices, mostly АД Астра at 0 %), **25 invoice lines with no VAT code**,
and the drinks split 18 % (chia) vs 5 % (aloe / gastro).
